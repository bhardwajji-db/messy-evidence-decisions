2. System Architecture & Pipeline

The system transforms noisy real-world data into structured judicial-grade determinations across 6 deterministic stages:

  1. Multimodal Ingestion: Ingests raw JPEG/PNG road photos, WAV citizen audio calls, official completion PDFs, and text grievances. Computes cryptographic SHA-256 hashes immediately.
  2. Extraction & Epistemic Classification: Extracts factual predicates and tags each claim as OBSERVED (direct physical evidence), INFERRED (derived severity), or ASSERTED (official claim).
  3. Spatio-Temporal Normalization: Cross-references landmarks, ward numbers (e.g. Gate 2 Road), and calendar timestamps across all media.
  4. Contradiction Matrix: Computes logical edges: CONTRADICTS (Red), CORROBORATES (Blue), or SUPPORTS (Green).
  5. Risk & Severity Calculation: Assigns HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW risk scores with explainable bulleted justifications.
  6. Human-in-the-Loop Review & Audit: Provides officer sign-off / override capabilities and generates printable certificates.
